What We Offer

Services

Each service is performed with the reverence and technical mastery that fine furniture demands.

Antique & Period Restoration

Complete conservation and restoration of 18th and 19th century furniture, including Federal, Georgian, Chippendale, and Queen Anne pieces.

Wood Refinishing

Hand-applied traditional finishes — French polish, oil, wax, and spirit varnish — that reveal the natural depth and grain of the wood.

Structural Repair & Joinery

Precise repair of broken tenons, loose joints, warped panels, and damaged casework using traditional and conservation-grade methods.

How We Work

The restoration process

01
Consultation

In-person or detailed photographic assessment. We discuss provenance, condition, and your goals for the piece.

02
Documentation

Full photographic record and condition report. We identify original construction details and previous repairs.

03
Conservation

Structural repair using reversible, conservation-grade methods. Damaged elements are stabilized or replaced in-kind.

04
Finishing

Traditional surface restoration — cleaning, color matching, and application of period-appropriate finishes.

05
Delivery

Final inspection, client walkthrough, and careful white-glove delivery with care instructions for long-term preservation.
